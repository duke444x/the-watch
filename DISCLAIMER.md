# Disclaimer

The Watch is experimental software demonstrating the use of the [Kraken CLI](https://github.com/krakenfx/kraken-cli) with autonomous AI agents.

## Paper trading only

This project uses Kraken CLI's local paper trading engine. No real funds are at risk. The `kraken paper` commands operate against a local state engine using live market prices but do not execute any real orders against the Kraken exchange.

## Not financial advice

The market analysis, trade decisions, and Bridge Logs produced by The Watch are generated by a large language model (Anthropic's Claude). Output is for demonstration and educational purposes only and does not constitute financial, investment, or trading advice. Nothing produced by this software should be relied on as the basis for any financial decision.
